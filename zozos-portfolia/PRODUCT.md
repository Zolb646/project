# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers scanning quickly for internship and junior software engineering candidates are the primary audience; they land here with limited time and need to be convinced fast that Zozo is worth a closer look. Engineering leads and future teammates are the secondary audience — they arrive after that first pass and dig into individual project pages for real technical evaluation (architecture, ownership, decisions made under real constraints).

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Zozo, a junior software engineer from Mongolia trained through Pinecone Academy's bootcamp and internship track, actively seeking internship and junior engineering roles. It exists to turn a resume line into proof: real, deployed projects with challenge/ownership/architecture/lessons write-ups that show Zozo can ship complete product work, not just tutorial exercises. Success is a recruiter or engineer engaging with a project in depth, then reaching out by email or pulling the resume.

## Positioning

Zozo already works like a team engineer, not a solo bootcamp grad performing skills in isolation. Every section should reinforce evidence of collaborative, production-shaped work — shared codebases, code review, git-based workflows, and features that had to fit existing systems — over a bare list of technologies.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: View Projects. Secondary CTA: Email Me and Download Resume, held at equal weight as fallbacks for a visitor not yet ready to click into a project.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: this is a junior engineer who already ships and collaborates like a team member, not someone who has only followed tutorials.
- Belief ladder: (1) this person builds real, complete products, not toy exercises — (2) this person has worked inside real team codebases and collaborative workflows, not only solo builds — (3) the craft and polish on display is strong enough to trust with production work — (4) therefore worth reaching out about an internship or junior role.
- Proof on hand: the four project case studies already in the codebase (Galexora, Sign Language Web, WordGym Mobile App, Quiz App), each with challenge, ownership, outcome, architecture, and lessons detail. No testimonials or external references are available yet.

## Brand Personality

Bold, confident, playful. The site already commits to a neo-brutalist identity — cream background, navy ink, hard drop shadows, thick borders, bright accent colors (orange, teal, yellow, violet), mono uppercase labels — and that direction should be trusted and extended rather than softened. No specific reference sites or anti-references were named; continue building on the existing visual system already established in the codebase rather than reaching for outside references.

## Design Principles

Evidence over claims: every section proves the "works like a team engineer" positioning through real project detail, not adjectives or a bare skills list. Scan-then-dive: respect a recruiter's fast first pass while rewarding an engineer's deeper look at individual project pages. Commit to the bold identity: the brutalist system is the differentiator against generic templated portfolios — lean into it rather than reaching for safer, quieter defaults. Frictionless path to contact: projects lead, but email and resume stay reachable everywhere, never gated behind another action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA baseline: solid color contrast, full keyboard navigation, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every animation, since the site already relies on entrance and hover motion throughout.
